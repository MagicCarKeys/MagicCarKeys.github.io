Before You Call

Do I need an appointment?

Yes for the service location. KeyProX is primarily mobile, and location visits are by confirmed appointment only. Call before visiting.

What information should I have ready?

Your vehicle year, make, model, location, the service you need, and whether you have a working key.

Can you help if all keys are lost?

For many supported vehicles, yes. Procedures vary by vehicle and ownership or authorization may need to be verified.

Do you work on German vehicles?

KeyProX supports many BMW, Audi, Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz and Porsche key and programming procedures. Call with the exact vehicle details to confirm.
