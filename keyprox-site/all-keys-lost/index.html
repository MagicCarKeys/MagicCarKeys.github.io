All Keys Lost

Lost every working key? KeyProX can help with key creation and programming for many supported vehicles.

When no working key remains

An all-keys-lost job can involve key cutting, transponder or proximity programming, immobilizer procedures and vehicle-specific security steps. The exact process depends on the vehicle.

What to have ready

Call with the vehicle details so we can confirm the correct procedure and availability.

  • Year, make and model
  • Your location
  • Whether the vehicle is locked
  • Proof of ownership or authorization when required

Mobile or appointment service

Many all-keys-lost jobs can be handled at the vehicle. Some procedures may be better suited to a confirmed appointment. Call first so we can direct you properly.
