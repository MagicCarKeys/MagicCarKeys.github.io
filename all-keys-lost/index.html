MOBILE ALL-KEYS-LOST SERVICE

Lost Every Key? You May Not Need a Tow

Losing the only car key can leave the vehicle completely unusable. For many supported vehicles, Magic Car Keys can come to the vehicle and create a replacement key even when there is no original key available.

The exact procedure depends on the vehicle. An all-keys-lost job may require vehicle identification, key-code or decoding procedures, key cutting, transponder programming, proximity-key or smart-key programming, immobilizer access and final testing.

For security-related procedures, we may need to verify ownership or authorization before accessing protected vehicle information or programming a new key.

  • Lost smart key or proximity key
  • Lost transponder key
  • Lost key fob or remote-head key
  • Replacement key cutting

  • Vehicle-specific key-code support when authorized

ALL KEYS LOST FAQ

Common Questions After Losing Every Car Key

Can you make a car key if I lost all my keys?

For many supported vehicles, yes. A new key can often be cut and programmed even when there is no working key left. The procedure depends on the vehicle's year, make, model and security system.

Can you make a car key without the original?

For many vehicles, the original key is not required. Vehicle identification, ownership verification, cutting and programming procedures may be needed.

Do I need to tow my car?

Not always. When the vehicle and procedure are supported, mobile service may allow us to create and program the replacement key at the vehicle location.

What information should I send first?

Send the year, make, model, location, VIN if available, whether the vehicle is locked, and whether any working key remains.

Can you replace a lost smart key or key fob?

Many smart keys, proximity keys, transponder keys and key fobs can be replaced and programmed, depending on vehicle compatibility.

Do I need proof of ownership?

For security-related procedures, proof of ownership or authorization may be required before protected vehicle information is accessed or a key is programmed.
